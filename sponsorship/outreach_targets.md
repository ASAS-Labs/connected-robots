## Suggested outreach order (practical)

1. Warm logos already on ears-conn.com (Nokia Bell Labs, Qualcomm, JPM, Fused, Gen Auto, PureCipher).  
2. NYC utilities + telecom + cloud (Con Edison, Verizon/AT&T, AWS/Azure/Google).  
3. Insurance/risk (Swiss Re, Munich Re, Guy Carpenter, Marsh) — matches programme “urban insurance intelligence.”  
4. EO/geospatial majors (Planet, Maxar, Esri, Fused upsell).  
5. Autonomy/chip (NVIDIA, Qualcomm deepen, Intel).  
6. Civic/in-kind (NYCEDC, city agencies) for amplification, not cash-first.

## Who is “good to reach out to” (role map)

Prefer **named intro via an organizer** when possible; otherwise ask reception for:

- **Academic / University Relations** — best for Platinum/Gold and brand-safe sponsorship  
- **Research Partnerships / Open Innovation** — best for demo tables and technical talks  
- **Corporate Responsibility / Community Impact (NYC)** — best for student scholarships & workshop support  
- **Product Marketing (robotics, geospatial, telecom, insurance innovation)** — best for Silver + exhibition  
- **Events / Conference sponsorship desk** — when a formal sponsor portal exists  

Avoid cold-mailing generic `info@` alone; use LinkedIn to identify the role above, then send the pack + short email (see `sample_outreach_email.md`).

## Notes

- Frame UN / NOAA / city entities as **partners or in-kind supporters**, not commercial sponsors, unless they have an explicit sponsorship mechanism.  
- Dollar amounts in the PDF are a starting proposal aligned with the IMBSA-style pack; adjust before sending if CCNY advancement or UNU Hub has required rates.  
- Always attach the PDF and link https://ears-conn.com.
