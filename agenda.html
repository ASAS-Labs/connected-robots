Embodied AI Bridging Autonomous and Remote Sensing for Reliable, Safe Connected Cities (EARS-CONN)

Conference Agenda

Two plenary days at the Grove School of Engineering, The City College of New York. Day 1 builds the sensing and embodied-AI foundations of Digital-Earth. Day 2 turns to urban applications and closes with the Innovation Stage, where selected startup and innovation applicants pitch for a prize. The hands-on workshop is a separate program on January 20–21.

Conference days: January 18–19, 2027 Venue: Grove School of Engineering, CCNY Format: One plenary track
